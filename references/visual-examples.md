# Visual composition examples

These are descriptions of three interface treatments used to explain [the main skill](../SKILL.md). They are starting points for composition, not fixed templates.

## Three planes and an oversized subject

In a product screen, large lettering sits behind an oversized product cutout. The subject crosses from a colored hero into a light purchase area. Price, color choices, and the purchase button sit in front. The overlap creates depth while the buying action stays clear.

## Editorial type and a consistent visual language

In a music player, artwork shapes the title treatment, progress line, and rounded controls. An oversized track title creates a focal point; the playback action remains recognizable. Carry the relationship between artwork, lettering, and controls through the screen when the product's tone calls for it.

## A background scene with content layered on top

In a weather screen, the sky and city form the setting. Current conditions, hourly forecasts, and details sit on readable foreground surfaces. Preserve legibility across changing image brightness and keep the scan order from current conditions to the forecast.
